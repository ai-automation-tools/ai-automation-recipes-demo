## 📌 Overview

A chronologically-numbered, slug-indexed cartoon collection covering **37 of the largest technology milestones in human history** — from the control of fire through modern artificial intelligence. Each image is a single unified cartoon editorial illustration depicting one milestone: the invention or breakthrough, with people using, building, or discovering it in an era-appropriate setting, plus readable embedded title text naming the milestone.

The collection is **plan-table driven and sequence-numbered** (eras span millions of years BCE to the present, so a 4-digit year won't work — the plan-table position is the ordering key instead): the milestones are generated in chronological order (oldest → newest), each run picks the first not-yet-generated milestone from the plan table, and each file is prefixed with that milestone's zero-padded `No.` so the order is visible everywhere. The goal is a fun, searchable, visually distinct cartoon record of how technology advanced across history.

> [!NOTE]
> The collection is **in progress**. The weekend-yearly-collections automation will add one new milestone at a time, picking the first `Pending` milestone slug from the plan table that isn't yet on disk.

## 🗂️ Folder Structure

```
Technology-Milestones/
├── README.md
├── Prompts/
│   └── generate-technology-milestones-collection.md   # workflow prompt + master image prompt + milestone plan
├── Images/
│   └── Technology-Milestones_<NN>-<Milestone-Slug>.png # one per milestone — 37 total target
└── Metadata/
    └── Technology-Milestones_<NN>-<Milestone-Slug>.md   # required sidecar, same stem as the image
```

`<NN>` is the **zero-padded chronological sequence number** (`01`–`37`) from the `No.` column of the plan table, and `<Milestone-Slug>` is the kebab-case slug from the same row (hyphens only, no underscore), e.g. `01-Control-of-Fire`, `18-Electricity`, `28-First-Computer`. The leading number sorts the collection oldest → newest on disk and in the gallery.

## ⚙️ How an Image Is Produced

1. The workflow agent reads [`Prompts/generate-technology-milestones-collection.md`](Prompts/generate-technology-milestones-collection.md), lists existing `Images/Technology-Milestones_<NN>-<Milestone-Slug>.png` files (stripping the `<NN>-` prefix to get the slug), and picks the first `Pending` milestone from the plan table whose slug isn't yet on disk.
2. The agent researches the selected milestone with **WebSearch** — what it is, why it matters, its era/date, the people and culture involved, and 3-6 era-appropriate visual details — using credible sources (Britannica, World History Encyclopedia, Smithsonian, Science Museum, Computer History Museum, IEEE, NASA, museum and university pages).
3. The master image prompt is filled in for that milestone and sent to the image model.
4. The output PNG saves to `Images/Technology-Milestones_<NN>-<Milestone-Slug>.png`.
5. A required metadata sidecar saves to `Metadata/Technology-Milestones_<NN>-<Milestone-Slug>.md` with the same stem.

## 🎨 Visual Style

- **Clean, friendly cartoon / editorial illustration** — bold confident outlines, flat-to-soft cel shading, warm expressive characters, simple readable shapes.
- **One unified scene** per milestone depicting the invention/breakthrough with people using or discovering it, never a rigid grid of panels or a disconnected sticker collage.
- **Readable embedded title text** naming the milestone (e.g. "ELECTRICITY"), legible at thumbnail size.
- **Era-appropriate setting, clothing, tools, materials, and props** grounded in research, with a couple of supporting references in the scene.
- **Short readable labels** only (1-5 words on signs, props, or panels). No long captions or fake paragraphs.
- **Wide landscape composition** with foreground human activity, midground breakthrough, and background era context.
- **No photorealism** — this is a cartoon. Avoid exact modern logos, exact brand/product trademarks, and photorealistic real-person likenesses; generic/stylized cues are fine.

## ✅ QA Checklist (per image)

- The milestone is recognizable at a glance.
- The image is a clean, friendly cartoon (not photorealistic).
- The embedded title text names the milestone and is readable and correctly spelled.
- The people are active and central; the breakthrough is clearly depicted.
- The era setting is historically plausible for the milestone.
- Labels are short, readable, and spelled correctly.
- No exact modern logos, brand/product trademarks, or photorealistic real-person likenesses.
- The image is a cohesive scene, not disconnected symbols.
- Filename matches `Technology-Milestones_<NN>-<Milestone-Slug>.png` (zero-padded `No.` prefix; slug uses hyphens only, no underscore).
- A metadata sidecar with the same stem was produced.
