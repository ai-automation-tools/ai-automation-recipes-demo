## Why It Is A Milestone

Bronze metallurgy combined copper with tin or other alloying materials to produce harder, more durable tools, weapons, vessels, and ornaments than earlier pure copper technologies. It also encouraged specialized workshops, mining, casting knowledge, and long-distance trade networks for scarce tin.
