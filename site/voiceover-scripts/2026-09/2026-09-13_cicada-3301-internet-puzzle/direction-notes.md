# Direction Notes — A Test With No Visible Prize

## Tone
Deadpan and even, like a true-crime narrator laying out evidence rather than selling a twist. Let the facts carry the weight — no vocal fry, no forced suspense. Cold curiosity, not dread.

## Pacing
- Target run time: 137 seconds
- Reading speed: ~160 wpm; slow to ~130 wpm on the final paragraph
- Beats / pauses: half-beat after "Then another." and "Then a QR code taped to a lamppost." (list rhythm, let each clue land); full beat after "Nobody who solved the puzzle ever said why."; full beat after "Then nothing."; slow down and land hard on the final two sentences

## Emphasis cues
- **highly intelligent individuals** — the quoted hook line, read it like it's being read off the original image
- **same private key** — the detail that makes the mystery feel engineered, not random
- **Then nothing.** — the turn; drop energy here before rebuilding into the theories
- **no visible prize** — the thesis of the whole piece, land it flat and certain

## B-roll / visual hooks (optional)
- Black 4chan-style image with white text materializing, letter by letter
- World map pins lighting up: Warsaw, Seoul, Paris, Sydney, Miami, Hawaii, Seattle
- Runic glyphs scrolling past, one page circled/highlighted as "solved"
- Calendar flipping January 2012 → 2013 → 2014 → April 2017 → present day, landing on a blank page

## Music / SFX (optional)
Low synth pulse under the first two paragraphs, minimal and mechanical. A soft dial-tone or modem blip under the phone-number line. Music thins out to near-silence on "Then nothing," then builds back in low for the kicker.
