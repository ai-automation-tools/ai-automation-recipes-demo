# Direction Notes — The Warship That Sank Before It Left the Harbor

## Tone
Dry, slightly incredulous documentary voice. Keep the sinking itself flat and matter-of-fact; let the irony do the work.
Lean in conspiratorially on "And somebody already knew." Deadpan through the inquest. Land the kicker quietly.

## Pacing
- Target run time: 135 seconds
- Reading speed: ~160 wpm; slow to ~130 wpm on the kicker
- Beats / pauses:
  - Beat after "Barely a hundred and twenty meters from shore."
  - Short clipped beats through "The lower gunports are open. Water pours in."
  - Full pause before and after "And somebody already knew."
  - Beat after "The ship sailed anyway."
  - Beat after "The blame drifted onto a dead man."
  - Long pause before the final line.

## Emphasis cues
- **thirteen hundred meters** — the whole joke of the story; hit the number
- **somebody already knew** — the turn from accident to negligence
- **three runs** — how fast the test went wrong
- **"If only His Majesty were at home!"** — read as a quoted aside, slightly lower
- **never even questioned** — the outrage line
- **promoted** — dry, let it sit
- **one mile** — kicker; soft and final

## B-roll / visual hooks (optional)
- Hook: on-screen number "1,300 m" over a map line from the palace to Tegelviken
- Departure: ornate carved stern, sails dropping, puff of cannon smoke for the salute
- Gust: ship tilting, water rushing through open gunports
- Stability test: stylised figures running across a deck, the hull rocking
- Inquest: candlelit council chamber, quill and paper
- Raising: 1961 salvage footage / pontoons breaking the surface
- Kicker: slow push-in on the preserved hull in the museum hall

## Music / SFX (optional)
Low, slow strings under the setup. Creaking timber and wind gust at the capsize, then a muffled underwater drop-out.
Strip music to near silence for "And somebody already knew." Return with a single sustained low note for the kicker.
