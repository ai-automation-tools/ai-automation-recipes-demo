# Direction Notes — The Spacecraft Lost to a Unit Label

## Tone
Calm and procedural, like a mission log read aloud. Quiet dread builds through the first half, then dry disbelief once the cause lands. No melodrama.

## Pacing
- Target run time: 98 seconds
- Reading speed: ~160 wpm; slow to ~130 wpm on the kicker
- Beats / pauses: beat after "wait for its signal to come back"; beat after "The signal never returns"; beat before "The software gave pound-seconds instead"; beat before "Six days after the orbiter was already gone"; long beat before the kicker.

## Emphasis cues
- **forty-nine seconds early** — first hint of trouble
- **fifty-seven** — against eighty survivable
- **pound-seconds** — the whole story in one word
- **four point four five** — the conversion factor
- **a label** — the kicker

## B-roll / visual hooks
- Engine burn and approach to a rust-red planet
- Orbiter disappearing behind Mars, silence
- Altitude bands: planned, survivable, actual
- Code file with a wrong unit
- Pound weight beside a newton scale
- Calendar flipping from the twenty-third to the twenty-ninth
