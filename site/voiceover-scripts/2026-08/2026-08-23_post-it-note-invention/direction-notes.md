# Direction Notes — The Adhesive That Wasn't Supposed to Work

## Tone
Wry and matter-of-fact, like someone telling a friend a story they still find a little funny. Low energy on the setup, a small lift each time the story turns ("Nobody bit," "It flopped"). Warmer and faster on the Boise Blitz turnaround, then settle into a dry, satisfied cadence for the kicker.

## Pacing
- Target run time: 113 seconds
- Reading speed: ~160 wpm; slow to ~130 wpm on the final paragraph, especially the last sentence
- Beats / pauses: brief beat after "Nobody bit." — let the failure land. Beat after "It flopped." before the pivot to Boise. Beat before "An adhesive built to fail." to signal the kicker is starting.

## Emphasis cues
- **strongest adhesive on Earth** — sets up the irony of what comes next
- **barely stuck at all** — the turn; land it flat, not dramatic
- **Nobody bit** — dry aside, almost a laugh line
- **90 percent** — the number that proves the pivot worked
- **never supposed to work** — final word of the piece; let it hang

## B-roll / visual hooks
- Macro shot of tiny adhesive spheres or a magnified sticky-note edge
- 1970s-style office/lab aesthetic — beakers, patent paper, seminar room
- A hymnal with paper slipping out mid-page-turn
- A stack of yellow sticky notes on a desk, one peeling and re-sticking

## Music / SFX
Light, curious plucked-instrument bed under the setup; a small tonal lift at the Boise pivot; drop the music out for the last two sentences so the kicker lands in near-silence.
