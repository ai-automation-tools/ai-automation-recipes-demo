### Prompt
> A majestic weeping willow tree entirely constructed from oxidized copper and bronze. The sweeping branches are chains of small gears dripping with glowing emerald liquid sap. The tree stands alone on a small island in a calm, dark lake reflecting the luminescent green sap. High contrast, cinematic composition, moody and surreal atmosphere.
