## Scene 3 — Cutting the Cord (scene-03.jpg)

> A modest small-town kitchen at evening in the 1910s, no wires on the walls or on the street beyond the window. On a shelf, a breadbox-sized receiver with a glass-domed copper coil and a small ticking meter beside it. A woman in an apron boils a kettle on a cooktop that glows from within with nothing visible feeding it. Through the window a tall tower stands over the town like a water tank. Warm interior, quiet wonder, faintly uncanny.

## Scene 4 — The Wireless War (scene-04.jpg)

> A muddy winter trench line in northern France at night under sleet, sandbags and duckboards. A young soldier in a wool greatcoat and steel helmet guards the base of a battered latticework power tower with a copper dome. Forty miles off, another tower's dome sits dark and cracked on the horizon; a nearby field hospital tent is lit only by candles. No visible violence, only cold aftermath. Somber, wide, the freezing dark.

## Scene 7 — The Slow Fuse (scene-07.jpg)
