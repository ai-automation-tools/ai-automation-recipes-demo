## Topic Description

Entertainment is eating itself alive — and audiences can't get enough. Hollywood keeps strip-mining the past for IP gold: reboots, sequels, live-action remakes, spin-offs of spin-offs. Streaming platforms bank on comfort economics, betting nostalgia clicks better than originality. But fans revolt when the execution misses, and the rare genuinely fresh hit breaks through anyway. Tonight we dig into why we keep going back, who profits from the loop, and whether there's any escape from the nostalgia machine.
