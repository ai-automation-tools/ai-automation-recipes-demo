# Should We Text the Aliens First?

**Date**: 2026-10-14
**Host**: Juno Partridge
**Cast**: Dr. Odalys Venn, Barnaby "Beacon" Okafor-Lund, Professor Thessaly Crane, Gus Fennimore

## About this episode

Should humanity beam a hello at nearby stars, or keep quiet and keep listening? A risk analyst, an allotment radio ham, a contrarian linguist and a retired cruise-ship entertainer fight it out while the host reads out a rowdy chat. It's funny, it's sharp, and one of the jokes lands harder than it has any right to.

## Highlights

- Odalys says you can't un-send a photon. Barnaby answers that anyone who crossed light-years has already survived their own worst habits.
- Thessaly argues nobody could understand the message, then admits aliens will judge it anyway: "That's every first date in history."
- The century of leaked TV and radar turns out to be our real first impression, and Gus defends the laugh track as a hello with a rhythm section.
- Gus describes the first person to clap in a nervous ship's lounge, and the debate goes quiet for a moment.
- Barnaby's dog Biscuit gets named as the ideal ambassador, and the host's jar of held thoughts runs full.
