# Boston Freedom Trail & Historic Neighborhoods Walk

[**→ Open the Interactive Map**](boston-freedom-trail-historic-walk_2026-07-15.html)

## What This Map Is

A curated, self-guided walking companion for one of America's most storied urban trails. The map combines the **12 most essential Freedom Trail sites** with **2 Beacon Hill gems** and **2 North End food stops**, giving you a full picture of colonial Boston, Federal-era residential neighborhoods, and the Italian-American enclave that grew up around the trail's North End section.

The red dashed line on the map traces the actual trail route from **Boston Common** through downtown, across the North End, over the Charlestown Bridge, and on to **Bunker Hill Monument** — a total of about 2.5 miles on mostly flat, brick-paved streets.

## Who It's For

- **First-time Boston visitors** who want to experience the city's founding history in a single day.
- **Families with kids** — the trail is stroller-friendly and each site has rangers or interpreters on site.
- **History & architecture enthusiasts** drawn to the oldest surviving buildings in the American colonies.
- **Day-trippers from nearby cities** (Providence, Hartford, Portland ME) looking for a walkable, full-day itinerary.

## How to Use the Map

1. **Start at Boston Common** (stop #1) and follow the numbered red markers north and east, then cross into Charlestown for the finale.
2. **Click any marker** for a photo, description, visitor tip, directions link, and the venue's website.
3. **Toggle categories** in the sidebar to show/hide the Freedom Trail route, Beacon Hill detour, or North End Eats.
4. **Click the sidebar stop list** to fly the map to that location.
5. The **trail route line** (dashed red) approximates the actual red-brick path embedded in Boston's sidewalks.

## Planning Your Day

**Half-day (3–4 hrs):** Boston Common → Granary → King's Chapel → Old South Meeting House → Old State House → Faneuil Hall. Finish with lunch at Quincy Market or the North End.

**Full day (6–8 hrs):** Complete all 16 stops, including the Charlestown leg (USS Constitution + Bunker Hill). Budget extra time for museum interiors and lines at Mike's Pastry.

**Getting There:** T (MBTA) to **Park Street** station (Green/Red Line) — drops you directly at Boston Common. Return from Charlestown via the **Community Boat Line** ferry (seasonal) or walk back across the bridge for a different view.

## Verify Before You Go

- **USS Constitution** timed-entry passes must be booked in advance at recreation.gov. Walk-up capacity is extremely limited in summer.
- **King's Chapel, Old South Meeting House, Old State House, Paul Revere House** charge admission; prices and hours change seasonally.
- **Mike's Pastry** and **Regina Pizzeria** have irregular holiday hours — check their websites if visiting on a holiday.
- **Bunker Hill Monument** elevator is out of service (open stairs only); confirm climb access at nps.gov.
- The Freedom Trail's outdoor sites (burying grounds, Boston Common, Copp's Hill) are free and open year-round.
