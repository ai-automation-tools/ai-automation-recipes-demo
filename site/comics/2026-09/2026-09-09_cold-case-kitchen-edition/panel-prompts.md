# Panel Prompts

## Panel 1
A colorful, modern 2D webcomic panel. A female cartoon character with short brown hair and oversized headphones stands dramatically in front of a corkboard covered in red yarn, sticky notes, and a photo of an empty plastic food container. She points at the board like a serious detective. Clean lines, flat colors.

## Panel 2
A colorful, modern 2D webcomic panel. The female cartoon character intently examines a dirty fork on a kitchen counter using a magnifying glass. In the background, a male cartoon character with glasses stands holding a clipboard, looking completely unamused and tired. Clean art style.

## Panel 3
A colorful, modern 2D webcomic panel. The female cartoon character dramatically shines an adjustable desk lamp directly at a fluffy orange cat sitting on a kitchen chair. The cat looks utterly bored and indifferent. Humorous, expressive cartoon style.

## Panel 4
A colorful, modern 2D webcomic panel. In the foreground, the male cartoon character with glasses washing a fork in the sink, looking sweating and guilty. In the background, the female character speaks into a vintage-style podcast microphone with great intensity. Clean vector art.

## Full Page Prompt
A 4-panel comic strip in a clean, modern 2D vector webcomic style, arranged in a 2x2 grid. The comic depicts two fictional cartoon characters, a woman with short brown hair who is obsessed with true crime podcasts, and her tired roommate, a man with glasses. They are dramatically investigating the 'crime' of missing leftover lasagna in their apartment, featuring a conspiracy red-string corkboard, a magnifying glass investigation of a dirty fork, interrogating a fluffy orange cat under a bright lamp, and the man guiltily washing the fork while she records a podcast about the mystery.
