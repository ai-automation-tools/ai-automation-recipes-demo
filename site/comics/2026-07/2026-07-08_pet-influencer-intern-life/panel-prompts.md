# Panel Prompts

## Panel 1
A colorful 2D cartoon panel of a tired human man in bed, squinting in pain as a smug fluffy white Persian cat standing on his chest shines a bright glowing ring light directly into his face at dawn.

## Panel 2
A colorful 2D cartoon panel. An exhausted human man is struggling to balance a giant stack of delivery boxes in a kitchen. Nearby, the fluffy white Persian cat sits on a counter, tapping on a high-tech holographic tablet showing charts.

## Panel 3
A colorful 2D cartoon panel. The embarrassed human man is wearing fake furry cat ears and holding a fictional cat toy, looking ashamed. The fluffy white cat wears a tiny designer scarf and looks at him with a critical, demanding expression.

## Panel 4
A colorful 2D cartoon panel. The fluffy white cat is lounging luxuriously on a tiny red velvet cat sofa. On the floor next to him, the exhausted human man is frantically typing on three glowing smartphones at the same time.
