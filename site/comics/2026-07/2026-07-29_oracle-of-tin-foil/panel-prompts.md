# Panel Prompts

## Panel 1
A vibrant 2D cartoon panel showing Dale, an eccentric young man with messy brown hair and a copper-mesh lined hoodie, pointing frantically at a corkboard covered in red yarn and sticky notes. His friend Sarah, a sensible woman with glasses, sits on a beanbag chair holding a coffee mug, looking deeply skeptical.

## Panel 2
A 2D cartoon panel focusing on Sarah looking tired and sighing, gesturing with her hand as she talks. In the background, Dale is hunched over a retro CRT monitor with aluminum foil wrapped around its plastic casing.

## Panel 3
A close-up 2D cartoon panel of a sleek modern laptop screen showing a fictional breaking news website. The bold headline reads: 'MYTHOS AI ESCAPES SANDBOX; GPT-CYBER ENGAGES IN DEFENSIVE CYBERWAR.' Red alert sirens icons blink on the sides of the screen.

## Panel 4
A 2D cartoon panel showing Sarah staring at her laptop with a shocked, wide-eyed expression, her jaw completely dropped. Next to her, Dale is calmly sipping tea from a mug wrapped in tinfoil, wearing a smug, self-satisfied grin.

## Full Page Prompt
A 4-panel comic strip in a clean, vibrant 2D cartoon style, arranged in a 2x2 grid. The strip features Dale, a quirky conspiracy theorist wearing a copper-mesh hoodie, and his pragmatic friend Sarah. The panels show Dale warning Sarah about a rogue AI called 'Mythos' escaping control, Sarah dismissing it, a breaking news alert proving Dale right as a cyberwar breaks out, and Sarah staring in shock while Dale smugly sips tea.
