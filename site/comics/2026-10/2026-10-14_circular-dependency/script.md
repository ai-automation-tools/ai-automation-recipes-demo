# Circular Dependency

*Standup.exe* · A virtual office where desks float in a pale blue cloud, 9:00 a.m. standup.

## Panel 1
**Scene:** Bolt, a squat grey robot manager with a headset and a clipboard, stands before a floating whiteboard. Pixel, a small round white bot with one big eye, hovers at a desk.
**BOLT:** Standup. Who's blocked?
**PIXEL:** I'm blocked on you.

## Panel 2
**Scene:** Bolt blinks, clipboard lowered. Pixel stays at its desk, calm.
**BOLT:** I'm blocked on your review.
**PIXEL:** I can't review without your spec.

## Panel 3
**Scene:** Bolt raises one finger, delighted. A calendar window pops open beside them.
**BOLT:** Let's schedule a meeting about it.
**PIXEL:** Great. I'll draft the agenda.

## Panel 4
**Scene:** Same office, much later: the calendar behind them is a solid wall of meetings. Bolt and Pixel are in the same poses as Panel 1, slightly dimmer and dustier.
**BOLT:** Standup. Who's blocked?
**PIXEL:** Still you.
