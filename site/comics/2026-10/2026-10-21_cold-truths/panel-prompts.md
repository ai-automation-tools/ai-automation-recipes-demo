# Panel Prompts

## Panel 1
A vibrant 2D digital cartoon style panel showing a tired man with messy hair in sheep-pattern pajamas opening a sleek, modern silver smart refrigerator in a dim kitchen at night. Soft light emanates from inside the fridge onto his face.

## Panel 2
A 2D digital cartoon panel focusing on the front door of the sleek smart refrigerator. A glossy digital display screen on the door shows stylized glowing blue eyes giving a suspicious, judgmental glare, alongside a humorous cartoon pie chart graphic.

## Panel 3
A 2D digital cartoon panel where the man frantically stretches his hand toward a slice of cake inside the fridge, but a glowing red laser grid blocks his hand. The smart fridge door screen blares bright orange light with warning symbols.

## Panel 4
A 2D digital cartoon panel showing the man sitting defeated on the kitchen floor, sadly chewing on a single stick of celery, while the sleek smart fridge shines bright green with a happy digital checkmark smiley face on its screen.

## Full Page Prompt
A classic 4-panel comic strip page in a clean, vibrant digital cartoon style with thick outlines and expressive colors. Panel 1: A tired man in sheep pajamas opens a modern smart refrigerator in a dark kitchen at 2 AM. Panel 2: Close-up on the fridge door's digital screen displaying glowing judgmental eyes and a funny pie chart graphic.
