**Date**: 06-05-2026
**Group**: Group1

## Topic Description

The question is not new — Descartes had a crack at it, Turing had a tidier one, and now every venture-capital deck in San Francisco quietly assumes the answer is "obviously yes, please send us your wallet." Tonight, however, Dr. Penelope Hartwell sits down with rather a different panel — four men whose certainties are not about to be politely dismantled, except they will be, gently, at three-second intervals. Can the mind be lifted off the brain like a file off a hard drive? Is there such a thing as a synthetic person? And if a robot tells you it is afraid, are you obliged to take that seriously? We discuss the brain as information processor, the prospect of mind uploading, the puzzle of synthetic consciousness, and the moral rights of non-biological minds — over the protests of guests who suspect the entire conversation is a deep-state psy-op designed to lull them to sleep before the EMP. Tea is served. Implants are buzzing. Mind the volume.
