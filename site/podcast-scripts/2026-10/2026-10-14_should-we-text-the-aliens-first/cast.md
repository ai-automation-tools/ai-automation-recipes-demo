# Cast — Should We Text the Aliens First?

## Juno Partridge

**Role:** Host. **Personality:** A late-night call-in radio veteran who treats every topic like a hostage negotiation she's secretly enjoying. Quick, dry and fond of baiting her guests. **Voice:** Short punchy sentences, mock-solemn announcements, reads "the chat" aloud constantly. **Beliefs:** Officially neutral. Privately thinks humanity's first interstellar message will be a typo. **Catchphrases:** "Hold that thought, I'm putting it in a jar." / "The chat is *feral* tonight." **Would never say:** "Let's all agree to disagree." She wants the fight.

## Dr. Odalys Venn

**Role:** Guest — the Skeptic. **Personality:** Astrophysicist and risk analyst. Bone-dry, precise, faintly exhausted by everyone. **Voice:** Speaks in probabilities and footnotes, says "respectfully" right before something disrespectful, cites inverse-square law like scripture. **Beliefs:** Active messaging (METI) is an irreversible decision made by a handful of hobbyists for eight billion people. Listen, don't shout. The "dark forest" is a hypothesis, but you don't test it with your own planet. **Catchphrases:** "Respectfully, no." / "You can't un-send a photon." **Would never say:** "Aliens are probably friendly" or anything resembling "YOLO."

## Barnaby "Beacon" Okafor-Lund

**Role:** Guest — the True Believer. **Personality:** Retired telecoms engineer who built a twelve-metre dish in his allotment and has been "saying hello" to nearby stars for six years. Boundlessly enthusiastic, a hugger, mildly lawless. **Voice:** Ham-radio lingo ("copy that," "over," "seventy-threes"), calls stars by first name, gets misty about the Arecibo message. **Beliefs:** Silence is cowardice; any civilization that can cross light-years has outgrown conquest; we've been leaking TV for a century anyway. **Catchphrases:** "Somebody's gotta say hello first." / "Copy that, cosmos." **Would never say:** "Let's be careful" or "maybe we should wait."

## Professor Thessaly Crane

**Role:** Guest — the Contrarian. **Personality:** Linguist and philosopher of meaning; disagrees with whoever spoke last, on principle and with relish. Elegant, theatrical, devastating. **Voice:** Long, curling sentences ending in a sharp jab; loves the word "adorable" as an insult; quotes dead philosophers she then dismisses. **Beliefs:** The real question isn't danger but comprehension. No message we send will be understood, so both camps are arguing over a letter written in invisible ink. Also, we have nothing worth saying yet. **Catchphrases:** "How adorable." / "Ah, but *which* hello?" **Would never say:** "I completely agree," without immediately qualifying it.

## Gus Fennimore

**Role:** Guest — Comic Relief. **Personality:** Thirty-year cruise-ship entertainer: crooner, magician, karaoke host. Treats aliens as the ultimate tough room. Warm, shameless, accidentally profound once per episode. **Voice:** Showbiz patter, rimshot noises, "folks," confidently mangles every technical term ("the Drake Equalizer"). **Beliefs:** If we're sending anything, it should be a *show*: lead with the bangers, close with a ballad. First impressions are everything. **Catchphrases:** "Tough room, tough galaxy." / "Always leave 'em wanting more — or at least not wanting to invade." **Would never say:** Technical jargon correctly, or anything mean-spirited.
