# Every Stone I Set

> A dry-stone waller looks back on a life of building walls on the hill, until the first wall he ever built is the one holding him up.

## [Intro]

## [Verse 1]
I was nine when he walked me up the hill,
a cart of fieldstone and a cold March sky.
He said, find the face and turn it out,
let the long side run across the line.
By the time the curlews came that spring
I could fill a gap and keep it dry.

## [Chorus]
Lay it heavy, lay it low,
leave the weather what it gets.
I was never made to stay,
but I'll stand in every stone I set.

## [Verse 2]
Forty winters on the open fell,
my knuckles split like bark in frost.
I built for men who never learned my name
and mended what the floods had tossed.
I raised a house for Annie by the ford
and counted every lamb we lost.

## [Chorus]
Lay it heavy, lay it low,
leave the weather what it gets.
I was never made to stay,
but I'll stand in every stone I set.

## [Interlude]

## [Verse 3]
Now my hands shake on the hammer,
and my daughter climbs the hill alone.
I sit all day on the wall I built at nine,
and for once it's holding me, stone on stone.

## [Final Chorus]
Lay it heavy, lay it low,
leave the weather what it gets.
I was never made to stay,
but I'll stand in every stone I set.

## [Outro]
Lay it heavy, lay it low.
