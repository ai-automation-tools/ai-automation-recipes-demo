# Soft Gravity

> Lying down and finally letting your weight belong to the earth.

## [Intro]
*(instrumental — tanpura drone, warm pad swell, fingerpicked guitar with tape echo, distant wind through grass; no vocals)*

Close your eyes, let go
Feel the bed rise slow
Every edge of you
Softens into blue

*(then instrumental fade — tanpura and pad decay, wind through grass, dissolving into room tone)*
