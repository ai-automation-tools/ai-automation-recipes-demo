# My Chickens Formed a Union

> A down-on-his-luck chicken farmer discovers his hens have organized, hired a shop steward named Darlene LaPlume, and won't lay a single egg until management caves on the dental plan — and eventually the 401(k).

## [Intro]

Now, y'all ain't gonna believe this...
I went out to the coop yesterday morning,
and there was a folded piece of paper tacked to the door!
