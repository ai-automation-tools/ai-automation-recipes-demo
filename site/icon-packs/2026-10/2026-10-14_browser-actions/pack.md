# Browser Actions

Twelve everyday browser controls as solid white glyphs on squircle tiles. Each tile has its own colour from a cool range of blues, indigos, violets and teals. The glyphs are filled shapes with square-cut ends, so they carry the same weight at 16 px and at 256 px. Use them for toolbars, launchers, shortcuts and docs.

**Formats:** every icon ships as vector source in `svg/`, a transparent 1024 px PNG in `png/`, and
a multi-size Windows `.ico` (16–256 px) in `ico/`.

**Using them:** drop an `.ico` onto a folder via Properties → Customize → Change Icon, or onto a
shortcut via Properties → Shortcut → Change Icon. The SVGs are the source art for web and app UI.

## Icons

- **Back** (`back`) — a thick left-pointing arrow.
- **Forward** (`forward`) — the same arrow mirrored to point right.
- **Reload** (`reload`) — a circular arc with an arrowhead closing the gap.
- **New tab** (`new-tab`) — a window outline with a tab on top and a plus inside.
- **Bookmarks** (`bookmarks`) — a ribbon with a notched bottom edge.
- **History** (`history`) — a clock face with two hands.
- **Downloads** (`downloads`) — a downward arrow landing on a tray.
- **Home** (`home`) — a house with a pitched roof and a door gap.
- **Stop** (`stop`) — a ring with a cross inside.
- **Find in page** (`find`) — a magnifying glass.
- **Full screen** (`fullscreen`) — four corner brackets pointing outward.
- **Print** (`print`) — a printer with paper going in at the top and coming out at the bottom.
